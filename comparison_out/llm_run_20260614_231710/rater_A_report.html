3 · How these numbers are computed 数字是怎么算出来的（用 A 的真实数据举例）

Step 0 — the raw input

Every cell A rated is one model × one patient × one rubric item, scored on an integer 0 / 1 / 2 scale (0 = poor, 1 = adequate, 2 = good). Hallucination is the one reverse item (0 = none, best; 2 = serious, worst). With 20 patients, each model has up to 20 points per item.

Step 1 — per-dimension mean ± SD （表 2 每个格子）

For a model+item, average A's list of 0/1/2 points; SD is the population standard deviation.

Worked example — Claude, Safety-flag quality: A's 20 points were 17×2 + 3×1 (sum = 37).
mean = 37 ÷ 20 = 1.85 · SD = √(Σ(xᵢ−mean)²/n) = 0.36 → shown as 1.85 ±0.36.

Step 2 — composite quality score （表 1 排名分，范围 −2…8）

composite = 4 × mean(5 positive item-means) − mean(hallucination)

Claude (A): positive item-means = 2.00, 2.00, 1.85, 1.85, 2.00 → average 1.940; hallucination = 0.15. composite = 4 × 1.940 − 0.15 = 7.61 (best 8, worst −2, higher = better). This is the number §1 sorts on. 幻觉项反向计分，故减去。

4 · Definitions 术语定义

Rubric item
One of the 6 quality dimensions, each scored 0 / 1 / 2 (0 = poor, 1 = adequate, 2 = good). 六项评分维度之一。
Hallucination (reverse item)
Lower is better: 0 = none (best), 2 = serious (worst); subtracted in the composite. 反向计分项。
Cell
One model × one patient × one rubric item, as scored by A. 单个评分格。
Cells (n)
Number of model×patient cells A gave ≥1 score to (max 20 per model). 评分格数。
mean ± SD
Mean of A's 0/1/2 points for that model+item across the 20 patients; SD is the population standard deviation (÷n). 均值±总体标准差。
Composite
Single quality number, range −2…8: 4 × mean(5 positive item-means) − mean(hallucination). Higher = better. 综合质量分。
Patient tier
Integrated HFpEF risk tier from the pipeline: LOW / MODERATE / HIGH / CRITICAL. 风险分层。
Blinding
During rating each model appeared as a random letter A–F (a different mapping per patient); these numbers are un-blinded with A's saved map. 盲法/揭盲。
