6 · How every number is computed 每个数字的算法（用 Claude 的真实数据举例）

Step 0 — the raw input

Each rated cell is one model × one patient × one rubric item, scored 0 / 1 / 2 by each of the 3 clinicians (0 = poor, 1 = adequate, 2 = good). Hallucination is reverse-scored (0 = none, best; 2 = serious, worst). Across 20 patients × 3 raters, each model has up to 60 points per item.

Step 1 — per-dimension mean ± SD （表 2 每个格子）

Pool all 0/1/2 points for that model+item across every rater and patient, then take the mean; SD is the population SD. E.g. Claude's mechanism-accuracy mean = 1.80 ±0.40.

Step 2 — composite (clinical quality, −2…8)

composite = 4 × mean(5 positive item-means) − mean(hallucination)

Claude: positive item-means = 1.80, 1.78, 1.62, 1.67, 1.75 → average 1.723; hallucination = 0.20. composite = 4 × 1.723 − 0.20 = 6.69 (best 8, worst −2).

Step 3 — the three 0–1 sub-scores

Quality = (composite − (−2)) / (8 − (−2))  →  Claude: (6.69+2)/10 = 0.869
Speed = 1 − latency / 120s  →  1 − 36.4/120 = 0.696
Economy = 1 − tokens / 10,000  →  1 − 4,485/10,000 = 0.551

Speed and Economy are floored at 0 (a model slower than 120s or above 10,000 tokens scores 0).

Step 4 — Total Mark (the ranking number)

Total = 0.70·Quality + 0.15·Speed + 0.15·Economy  (the research preset weights).

Claude: 0.70×0.869 + 0.15×0.696 + 0.15×0.551 ≈ 0.795 → shown as 0.796 in §1 (the leaderboard computes it from full-precision sub-scores, so the last digit differs slightly from this rounded-input version). Change the weights and the order changes — e.g. a speed-heavy "bedside" preset lifts Qwen and sinks Kimi.

Step 5 — inter-rater reliability (§3)

ICC(2,k) is a two-way random-effects, average-measures intraclass correlation computed per dimension over the 120 model×patient subjects rated by all 3 clinicians: it asks how much of the score variance is real between-report differences vs. rater disagreement (1 = perfect agreement, 0 = none). Mean Spearman ρ is the average rank-correlation across the three rater pairs — a simpler sanity check. Both are computed on raw points, not the means.

7 · Definitions 术语定义（每个指标的确切含义）

Rubric item
One of the 6 quality dimensions a clinician scored. Each is an integer 0 / 1 / 2 (0 = poor, 1 = adequate, 2 = good). 六项评分维度之一。
Hallucination (reverse item)
The one item where lower is better: 0 = no fabricated/incorrect inference (best), 2 = serious hallucination (worst). It is subtracted in the composite. 反向计分项。
Cell / rating
One model × one patient × one rubric item, scored by one clinician. 20 patients × 6 models × 3 raters = 360 possible cells per item. 单个评分格。
n
Number of rated cells behind a model's row = model × patient × rater combinations that received ≥1 score (max 60 = 20 patients × 3 raters). 参与计算的评分数。
mean ± SD
Arithmetic mean of the 0/1/2 points for that model+item, pooled over all raters and patients; SD is the population standard deviation (÷n). 均值±总体标准差。
Composite
Single clinical-quality number, range −2…8: 4 × mean(5 positive item-means) − mean(hallucination). Higher = better. 综合质量分。
Quality score
Composite rescaled to 0–1: (composite + 2) / 10. 质量分（0–1）。
Speed score
max(0, 1 − latency / 120 s) using mean per-patient latency; 0 at ≥120 s. 速度分。
Economy score
max(0, 1 − tokens / 10 000) using mean per-patient total tokens; 0 at ≥10 000. 经济性分。
Total Mark
Weighted blend w_Q·Quality + w_S·Speed + w_E·Economy; the research preset uses 0.70 / 0.15 / 0.15. 总分。
Preset
A named weighting of Quality/Speed/Economy — research (0.70/0.15/0.15), bedside (0.50/0.40/0.10), economy (0.50/0.20/0.30). 权重预设。
Latency
Mean wall-clock seconds per patient for the model's reasoning + safety calls (objective, from the generation run — not rated by clinicians). 每例平均耗时。
Tokens
Mean total tokens (prompt + completion) per patient (objective, from the generation run). 每例平均 token 数。
ICC(2,k)
Intraclass correlation, two-way random-effects, average-measures — agreement among the 3 raters on a dimension (1 = perfect, 0 = none, may go slightly negative). 评分者间一致性。
Spearman ρ
Mean rank-correlation across the 3 rater pairs — a simpler agreement check. 秩相关。
Patient tier
Integrated HFpEF risk tier assigned by the pipeline: LOW / MODERATE / HIGH / CRITICAL. 风险分层。
Blinding / un-blinding
Each rater saw models as random letters A–F (a different mapping per patient); numbers here are un-blinded with each rater's saved map. 盲法/揭盲。
